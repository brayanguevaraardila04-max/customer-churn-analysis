# Findings — Synthetic customer churn case study

The dataset includes **2,000 synthetic customers**. Overall churn is **17.35%**.

| Contract type | Customers | Churn rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Month-to-month | 1,094 | 23.40% |
| One year | 550 | 12.73% |
| Two year | 356 | 5.90% |

## Interpretation
Month-to-month customers show the highest observed churn in this simulated dataset. A possible business follow-up would be to test targeted onboarding or retention offers for this segment, then measure impact through a controlled experiment.

## Limitations
The dataset was generated artificially with assumptions that influence churn probability. These results are **not** evidence about an actual telecom company, and correlations do not establish causation.
